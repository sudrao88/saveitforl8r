# TestFlight Description — SaveItForL8R

## App Store Description

SaveItForL8R is your AI-powered personal second brain. Capture text, images, PDFs, and files — then let Google Gemini automatically enrich, tag, summarize, and organize everything for you. Your data is encrypted locally and optionally synced to Google Drive across all your devices.

## What to Test (Build v60)

Welcome to the SaveItForL8R beta! Here's what we'd love your feedback on:

### Core Capture
- Save text notes, images, PDFs, and files using the quick-note bar or full editor
- Attach multiple images and documents to a single memory
- Auto-capture your location when saving a note
- Share content from other apps directly into SaveItForL8R via the iOS Share Extension

### AI Enrichment (Google Gemini)
- Watch your notes get automatically enriched with summaries, tags, and context
- Content-type detection: recipes, events, travel plans, books, movies, jobs, and more
- Each content type gets specialized enrichment (e.g., ingredients for recipes, showtimes for movies)

### Brain Search
- Tap the brain icon to ask natural language questions across all your memories
- Get conversational answers with source citations linking back to your notes
- Multi-turn follow-up questions for deeper exploration

### Moments
- AI-synthesized collections that group related memories into coherent narratives
- Supports trip planning, event prep, research, comparisons, health journeys, and more
- Browse the Moments bubble strip on the main feed

### Calendar & Todos
- Automatic detection of dates and events from your notes
- Calendar agenda view for upcoming events
- Action items extracted and organized into a todo list

### iOS Widget
- Quick-note widget for capturing thoughts without opening the app

### Sync & Security
- Sign in with Google to enable encrypted Google Drive backup
- Verify sync works across devices
- All data is AES-256 encrypted at rest on your device

### Known Considerations
- AI enrichment requires an internet connection; notes save offline and enrich when back online
- First sync may take a moment if you have many existing notes
- The Share Extension works best with text and single images

### Feedback Areas
We especially want to hear about:
1. Reliability of AI enrichment — do summaries and tags feel accurate?
2. Brain Search quality — are answers relevant and well-sourced?
3. Sync behavior — does cross-device sync work smoothly?
4. Overall performance — any lag, crashes, or UI glitches?
5. Widget usability — is the quick-note widget responsive and easy to use?

Thank you for testing SaveItForL8R! Report issues via TestFlight feedback or shake your device to send a report.
